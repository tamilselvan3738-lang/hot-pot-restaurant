The Art of the First Boil

Long before the first cut of Wagyu enters the pot, a quiet alchemy takes place. The initial simmer awakens sleeping fats, releases encapsulated botanical oils, and signals that the feast has officially begun.

98.5°C Optimal Simmer
01

Awakening the Botanicals (65°C - 75°C)

Subtle warmth coaxes dried Angelica sinensis, red dates, and star anise to slowly hydrate, permeating the nascent stock with delicate herbal honey notes.

02

The Ring of Perl Pearls (85°C - 92°C)

Concentric bubbles form an unbroken ring along the hand-hammered brass perimeter. The surface transforms into a shimmering ruby mirror.

03

The Rolling Bloom (98°C - 100°C)

A vigorous roll unfurls from the center outward. Hot pot steam billows skyward carrying intense toasted sesame and aged pepper aromas to everyone at the table.

04

The Culinary Threshold (Ready to Immerse)

Broth viscosity reaches peak suspension. Thin ribbons of A5 beef will cook to transcendent tenderness in precisely 8 to 12 seconds.
