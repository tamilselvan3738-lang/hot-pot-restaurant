Arrival & Transit Logistics

Effortless Arrival & Parking Guidance

Arrive with grace. Enjoy white-glove valet service, validated subterranean parking with EV charging, or covered high-speed transit links.

White-Glove Valet Parking

Available at our private Porte-Cochère on Imperial Dragon Way. Complimentary for VIP Gold Broth members; $12 flat rate for dining guests with ticket validation. Attendants greet you upon arrival.

Underground Heated Parking & EV Fast Docks

Direct elevator access from the Celestial Pavilion underground garage (Levels B1 & B2). First 3 hours validated for restaurant patrons. Equipped with 12 Tesla Superchargers and universal CCS plugs.

Rapid Metro & Rail Access

Dragon Gate Station (Red & Gold lines). Take Exit B and walk 180 meters along the covered Imperial Glass Arcade directly to our sanctuary elevator lobby.

Universal Step-Free Accessibility (ADA)

Zero-step entrance from curb to dining pavilion. Broad elevators, tactile wayfinding, wheelchair-proportioned booths, and accessible private washrooms. Wheelchair escort available upon request.

Rideshare Drop-Off Point

Specify "88 Imperial Dragon Way – Valet Drop" on Uber or Lyft for direct covered pavilion drop-off right at our host desk.
