Our Origin & Identity • Est. 1998

Born of Riverbank Fire, Forged in Golden Harmony

In the late autumn of 1998 along the misty, cliff-hung riverbanks of Chongqing, our culinary pilgrimage began around a solitary hand-hammered copper cauldron. Boatmen and traveling merchants gathered at twilight, drawn by the intoxicating aroma of slow-simmered beef tallow, wild Sichuan peppercorns, and roasted chilies dancing over glowing charcoal.

The Celestial Cauldron was founded on a singular conviction: that hot pot is not merely an eating method, but a sacred communal ritual of connection. In an increasingly fragmented world, gathering around a shared, boiling broth dissolves social boundaries, inviting family, friends, and strangers to share the same heat, rhythm, and joy.

Cultural Heritage

A Thousand Winters Around the Shared Hearth

From ancient bronze cauldrons over charcoal pits to courtly imperial banquets, hot pot is a living ritual of human fellowship and warmth.

Circa 800 AD • Tang Dynasty

The Bronze Hearth of Wei Lu

Early Chinese scholars and villagers gathered during winter solstices around bronze vessels called Ding (鼎). Meat, mountain mushrooms, and wild ginger were simmered together in clean spring water, creating the communal ritual of Wei Lu (围炉)—encircling the stove for protection against frost and spirit replenishment.

19th Century • Chaotianmen Docks

The Alchemy of Dockside Mala

Along the rugged junctions of the Yangtze and Jialing rivers, boat trackers and dockers invented the bold, tallow-based hot pot. To repel damp river chill and preserve food, they simmered cattle tallow with whole dried chilies, mountain peppercorns, and star anise in divided clay pots, planting the seeds of classical Sichuan Mala broth.

围炉聚饮

"Social distinctions vanish in the rising vapor; kings and rivermen alike must wait for the broth to boil."

18th Century • Qing Dynasty Court

The Thousand Elders Feast

Emperor Qianlong revered hot pot so deeply that he hosted the historic "Banquet of a Thousand Elders" inside the Forbidden City, serving over 1,550 ornate silver and copper cauldrons brimming with game birds, ginseng, and wolfberry elixirs to elders from across the empire—cementing hot pot as the pinnacle of imperial hospitality.

一鼎乾坤

A single cauldron encompassing Heaven, Earth, Fire, and Water in delicious harmony.

Gastronomic Doctrine

The Equilibrium of Five Harmonious Flavours

Rooted in ancient Taoist culinary metaphysics, our broths embody Wu Wei Tiao He (五味调和)—the harmonious union of opposing forces in one boiling cauldron.

"In the cauldron, fire and water cease their war; they marry to create life, aroma, and memories."
Doctrine I • Balance

Wu Wei Equilibrium (五味)

Sweetness from rock sugar and dates balances the pungency of ginger and Sichuan peppercorns, while deep umami from marrow grounds the vibrant heat. No single note screams; every flavor sings in chorus.

Yin-Yang Twin Cauldron Alchemy
Doctrine II • Resonance

The Symphony of Ma & La (麻辣)

True Mala is not punishing heat. It is an intricate resonance: Hanyuan tribute peppercorns induce a delicate 50Hz tingling vibration on the palate, opening taste receptors to the fruity, roasted notes of 7 chili varietals.

Doctrine III • Patience

The 18-Hour Slow Simmer (慢炖)

Time is our most indispensable ingredient. Cattle femur marrow bones and old farm chickens are simmered at an unbroken 98°C for eighteen hours, emulsifying collagen into a velvet broth that coats the palate.

Doctrine IV • Temperance

Botanical Wholesomeness (药膳)

To balance the warmth of chili and tallow, we infuse high-altitude medicinal botanicals—Yunnan wild morels, goji berries, angelica sinensis, and codonopsis—ensuring gentle digestion and deep bodily vitality.

Chao Liao (The 6-Hour Spice Bloom)

Conducted every dawn inside hammered copper kettles. 36 individual spices—including hand-cracked star anise, aged tangerine peel, and dried cardamom pods—are stirred continuously by hand in pure clarified tallow over low flame to extract delicate volatile aromas without scorching.

02

Triple-Filter Collagen Clarification

Our golden herbal chicken and mushroom broths undergo three separate filtering stages through fine Japanese silk sieves, separating impurities while preserving natural emulsified lipids for crystal-clear velvet richness.

03

The 1.8mm Precision Wagyu Shave

Using hand-forged Sakai yanagiba knives, master carvers slice chilled Miyazaki A5 ribeye into gossamer 1.8mm ribbons designed specifically for the legendary Qi Shang Ba Xia (七上八下 - "Seven Up, Eight Down") 8-second flash cook.
