CRITICAL GUEST ALLERGY ADVISORY:

Our dining room operates active boiling cauldrons and a communal sauce bar where peanuts, tree nuts, sesame seeds, soybeans, shellfish, crustaceans, fish, eggs, and wheat are regularly handled. While our team enforces strict separation protocols, airborne vapors and aerosolized chili oils cannot be guaranteed 100% allergen-free. Please notify our head waitstaff immediately upon arrival if you have severe anaphylactic allergies.

We believe extraordinary hot pot is born strictly from uncompromising ingredient integrity. We source directly from certified ethical growers and producers:

  • Australian Full-Blood Wagyu: Sourced from Darling Downs, grain-fed for 450 days to guarantee verified MBS 8-9+ marbling.
