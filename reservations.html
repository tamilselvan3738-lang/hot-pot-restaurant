Hospitality Covenant & Trust

Reservation & Cancellation Policy

Because our broths require up to 18 hours of simmering and Miyazaki Wagyu is hand-portioned to order, we ask that our patrons honour this communal dining covenant.

4-Hour Courtesy Cancellation

Plans shift gracefully. You may modify your reservation time, party size, or cancel entirely without fee up to 4 hours prior to your scheduled sitting. One-tap cancellation is available in your digital confirmation email.

15-Minute Holding Guarantee

Your table is held for exactly 15 minutes past your reserved time. If you arrive early, relax in our Imperial Tea Lounge where complimentary hot chrysanthemum tea and lotus root crisps are served tableside.

VIP Salon & Group Deposits

For Private Dining Salons and parties of 8 or more, reservation deposits are 100% refundable up to 48 hours prior to seating. Dietary changes for large feasts may be updated up to 6 hours before arrival.
