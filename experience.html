Culinary Autonomy

You Are The Master Chef Of Your Cauldron

Forget rigid culinary formalities. Here, you dictate texture, cook times, and flavor combinations. Follow our master five-stage path to achieve hot pot perfection.

01

The Flame Ignition

Your server ignites the cauldron, bringing 18-hour master elixirs to a precise 98°C rolling simmer. Inhale the initial release of star anise, ginger, and angelica.

02

The Sauce Alchemy

Visit our 18-condiment Alchemist Bar. Coat your palate with velvety roasted sesame paste, garlic-scallion puree, and homemade sizzling chili oil.

03

The Sacred Swish

"Qi-Shang-Ba-Xia" (7 Up, 8 Down). Submerge delicate Wagyu ribbons for exactly 8 seconds until the snowflake fat renders into luscious silk.

04

Garden Infusion

Introduce Yunnan morel mushrooms, crispy bean curd sheets, and sweet lotus roots. Let them simmer gently, drinking in the savory meat juices.

05

The Slurp & Savor

Conclude by dropping hand-pulled Sanuki udon or glass noodles into the now deeply concentrated elixir. Slurp enthusiastically with tableside broth toasts.

Tableside Mastery
